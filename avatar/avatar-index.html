Someone to stand in your garden. A flower to wear, and a few things to carry.

    the beginning

    What shall we call them? You can leave this empty and they will simply be your gardener.

    They will stand in your personal garden, and in the shared garden they stand beside the flower you planted that day.

    choose a gardener

    Eight skins and twelve hair colours: eight the head grows and four it does not. The hair is what shows inside the flower, so it is a fringe and a piece down each cheek.

    choose your flower

    Six of the eight ring the face like a hood. The tulip and the lavender have no ring of petals to sit inside, so they GROW instead: a plain cowl and the flower on a short stem out of the crown.

    choose what they wear

    One hue, which every other tone on the figure is derived from, and one outfit in one of three colours. Tap it again to take it off and leave them plain.

    Colour

    choose what they carry

    Three of each, in three colours. Tap the one they are wearing again to take it off. Nothing here is earned, and nothing is tied to a species.
