YOUR DAILY GRATITUDE PRACTICE

Grow your Gratitude, one bloom at a time.

A gentle digital space for your thoughts. Reflect by yourself, share them with the community, or gather a few into a bouquet to give to someone else.

What is it?

One practice, three places.

Gratitude Garden is one practice, three ways, grown from the same eight flowers.

The Personal Garden is a private journal where your words choose your flower. The Shared Garden is a public meadow anyone can plant in, refreshed daily. The Bouquet Builder lets you gather, wrap, and send a bouquet with a note only they can open.

Two of these you keep and return to. One you give away. Reflect, share, give: the same eight flowers, three different ways to mean something.

One account joins them up. It claims the username you plant under, carries your personal garden between devices, keeps the day by day history of everything you have grown, and holds the friends you can send a bouquet to. You can also skip it entirely and use any of the three in this browser alone.

Making the most of it

Gratitude tips

Do it anytime, anywhere

No desk or routine required. Plant during lunch, add to the meadow on the bus, tie a bouquet before bed.

Be specific, not general

"The coffee tasted perfect today" grows a truer bloom than "I am grateful." Specific beats vague, everywhere you write.

Keep some, give some

Save your garden as a keepsake, plant in the meadow for company, send a bouquet when someone comes to mind.

Trust what you feel

Your words choose the flower. In a bouquet, you choose the stems. Either way, the meaning is yours.
